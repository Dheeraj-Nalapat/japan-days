## 🗓️ Day-by-Day Itinerary

### 📍 OSAKA — Base: Hommachi (Oct 11–15, 4 nights + 1 Koyasan)

> Style: quiet/history days · food nights
> Stay: Hostel private room, Hommachi area (~₹2,750/night)

---

**Day 1 — Oct 11 (Arrival)**

Arrive KIX 18:25 local. Take Nankai Rapi:t or Haruka Express to city (~¥1,450–1,880). Check in Hommachi.

- 🌙 **Dotonbori** — first taste of Osaka's neon chaos. Walk Ebisu-bashi bridge, take in the Glico sign.
- 🕯️ **Hozenji Yokocho** _(Hidden gem)_ — duck into this lantern-lit mossy alley right beside Dotonbori. Moss-covered Fudo Myo-o statue, locals splash water on it for blessings. Feels like a different century.
- 🍜 **Dinner**: Takoyaki at Kukuru + walk Dotonbori street stalls

---

**Day 2 — Oct 12 (Osaka City — History + Hidden)**

- 🌅 **Osaka Castle Grounds + Nishinomaru Garden** (¥200) — arrive before 9am. Walk the moat paths, Sakuramon Gate, autumn foliage starting in the garden. No tower entry — grounds are the real experience.
- ☕ **Nakazaki-cho** _(Hidden gem — very low crowds)_ — Osaka's bohemian quarter. Old wooden machiya townhouses, vintage coffee shops, indie bakeries. Almost zero foreign tourists. Best on a slow afternoon wander.
- 🐟 **Kuromon Ichiba Market** — Osaka's kitchen. Fresh sashimi, grilled scallops, wagyu skewers straight from the stall. Lunch here.
- 🌆 **Shinsekai** from 6pm — retro neon district. Kushikatsu at Daruma (deep-fried skewers, no double dipping the sauce). Walk under the Tsutenkaku Tower.

---

**Day 3 — Oct 13 (Nara + Kobe Day Trip)**

_Both are half-days — combine into one long day._

**Morning → Nara (40 min, Kintetsu line from Namba, ¥680)**

- 🦌 **Nara Deer Park** — hundreds of free-roaming deer, feed them shika senbei crackers (¥200/pack)
- 🏛️ **Todai-ji Temple** — giant Buddha hall, largest wooden building in the world (¥600)
- 🌿 **Isuien Garden** _(Hidden gem)_ — strolling garden that uses Todai-ji's roof as "borrowed scenery." Most visitors walk right past it. ¥1,200.
- Leave Nara by 1pm

**Afternoon → Kobe (30 min from Nara via Osaka, or ~55 min direct)**

- 🌊 **Kitano Ijinkan** — Western-style foreign residences on the hillside, quiet streets with sea views
- 🥩 **Kobe Beef dinner** — try Kobe beef teppanyaki at a local restaurant in Kitano or Sannomiya area. Budget ¥3,000–5,000 for a proper set.
- Back to Hommachi by 9pm

---

**Day 4 — Oct 14 (Koyasan Overnight)**

_Take the afternoon/evening train up. Koyasan is a UNESCO mountain monastery town 2hrs from Osaka._

- Morning: free in Osaka — **Denden Town** (Osaka's Akihabara, anime/electronics) or **Tsuruhashi Koreatown** (yakiniku, kimchi market)
- **Afternoon (3–4pm)**: Nankai Koya Line from Namba → Gokurakubashi → cable car → Koyasan (~2hrs, ¥2,000)
- 🌲 **Okunoin Cemetery** — walk the 2km lantern-lit path through ancient cedar trees past 200,000 tombstones at dusk. One of the most atmospheric places in Japan.
- 🛕 **Shukubo (temple lodging)** — overnight stay in a Buddhist monastery. Tatami rooms, vegetarian shojin ryori dinner, futon bedding.
- 🌄 Next morning: monks' prayer ceremony at 6am (optional, highly recommended) → vegetarian breakfast → leave by 9am

---

**Day 5 — Oct 15 (Back from Koyasan + Rest)**

- Back in Osaka by 11am
- Easy half-day: **Expo '70 Park** (Tower of the Sun sculpture, autumn silver grass fields, ¥260) or rest
- Last Osaka dinner: **Tsuruhashi Koreatown** — yakiniku BBQ 🥩 or back to Dotonbori for anything you missed
- Pack and prepare to move to Kyoto tomorrow
