# 🎬 Makoto Shinkai — Real Life Locations

**聖地巡礼 · Seichi Junrei · Anime Pilgrimage**

> Every location below is a real, visitable place in Japan. Shinkai is known as the _"god of background art"_ — his team replicates real locations with near-photographic accuracy. Standing at these spots after watching the films is a genuinely different experience.

## 🌟 YOUR NAME (君の名は) — 2016

_Taki lives in Tokyo. Mitsuha lives in the fictional town of Itomori. The film cuts between both worlds._

---

### 📍 Tokyo Locations

**1. Suga Shrine Steps (須賀神社)**

- 📌 Yotsuya, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Yotsuya-Sanchome Station (Marunouchi Line) — 5 min walk
- The most famous anime pilgrimage spot in Japan. The Suga Shrine steps are where Taki and Mitsuha finally reunite in the film's closing scene — the actual location matches the film almost perfectly.
- Free entry, outdoor staircase
- 💡 _Go early morning (7am) for an empty staircase shot — by 10am fans queue for photos_

**2. Yotsuya Station**

- 📌 Yotsuya, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Yotsuya Station (JR Chuo Line)
- Taki and Okudera-senpai meet up at Yotsuya Station for their date — the above-ground platform view appears repeatedly in the film
- Free to view from street level

**3. National Art Center Tokyo (国立新美術館)**

- 📌 7-22-2 Roppongi, Minato-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Nogizaka Station (Chiyoda Line) — direct underground exit
- The restaurant "Salon de the Rond" on the 2nd floor is where Taki and Okudera-senpai dine on their date — the brown-lined walls and glass-panelled ceiling appear in the film. Going inside the facility is free.
- The restaurant opens at 11am; free to enter the art centre itself

**4. Tokyo City View — Roppongi Hills**

- 📌 6-10-1 Roppongi, Minato-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Roppongi Station (Hibiya / Oedo Line)
- Taki and Okudera-senpai visit this observation deck during their date. Entry costs ¥1,800.
- Walkable from National Art Center in ~15 min

**5. Shinjuku Station & Surroundings**

- 📌 Shinjuku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Shinjuku Station
- Taki's neighbourhood and commuting hub. The west exit walkways, east exit scramble, and surrounding streets all appear throughout the film
- Free

**6. Taki's Apartment Area — Yoyogi**

- 📌 Near NTT Docomo Yoyogi Building, Shibuya-ku
- 🚇 Shinjuku Station south exit / Yoyogi Station
- The NTT Docomo Yoyogi Building appears in multiple scenes; Sangūbashi Park and the surrounding streets of Yoyogi appear in Taki's daily life sequences

---

### 📍 Outside Tokyo — Itomori Model Locations

**7. Lake Suwa (諏訪湖) — Nagano**

- 📌 Suwa City, Nagano Prefecture
- 🚄 2h from Tokyo (Azusa Limited Express from Shinjuku)
- The Lake Suwa area is one of the key locations that inspired Itomori — Shinkai's home region of Nagano
- Suwa Grand Shrine (Suwa Taisha) is the model for Itomori's shrine
- The lake at dusk looks almost identical to the film
- ⚠️ _Not on your current itinerary — would require a separate day trip from Tokyo_

**8. Hida / Furukawa — Gifu**

- 📌 Hida City, Gifu Prefecture
- Hida in Gifu is one of the rural models for Itomori — Your Name pilgrims visit here for the old wooden townhouses and mountain-valley setting
- 2.5h from Osaka or Nagoya by limited express
- ⚠️ _Not on your current itinerary_

## 🌧️ WEATHERING WITH YOU (天気の子) — 2019

_The entire film is set in Tokyo — a rainy, flooded version of the city you can explore on a clear October day._

---

### 📍 Tokyo Locations

**9. Koenji Hikawa Shrine — The "Rooftop Shrine" (高円寺氷川神社)**

- 📌 3-58-12 Koenji-Minami, Suginami-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Koenji Station (JR Chuo Line) — 10 min walk
- In the film the shrine appears to be on a building rooftop — in reality it is Koenji Hikawa Shrine. The ema wooden plaques for weather prayers are a direct reference.
- Free entry
- 💡 _Koenji is also one of Tokyo's best indie/vintage neighbourhoods — combine with the shrine visit_

**10. Tabata Station South Exit (田端駅南口)**

- 📌 Tabata, Kita-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Tabata Station (JR Yamanote Line)
- Tabata Station South Exit is one of the most-visited real-life locations from Weathering With You — the station exterior and surrounding streets match the film closely
- Free

**11. Yoyogi Kaikan Building (代々木会館)**

- 📌 1-36-1 Tomigaya, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo (near Yoyogi Park)
- 🚇 Yoyogi-Hachiman Station (Odakyu Line)
- Yoyogi Kaikan is one of the prominent real-life locations in Weathering With You — the old rundown building where Hina and her brother live is modelled on this
- View from street; building exterior only

**12. McDonald's in Shinjuku**

- 📌 East side of Shinjuku Station, Tokyo
- 🚇 Shinjuku Station
- The McDonald's from Weathering With You has become a popular photo spot for fans — the specific branch near Shinjuku east exit appears in the early scenes where Hodaka first arrives in Tokyo
- Obviously free to enter (and grab a McFlurry 🍦)

**13. Shinjuku — New Metropolitan Footbridge**

- 📌 West Exit, Shinjuku Station, Tokyo
- 🚇 Shinjuku Station west exit
- The New Metropolitan Footbridge in Shinjuku West Exit area is the background model for the climax scene where the giant worms cover the Tokyo sky — from the footbridge you can overlook Shinjuku's skyscraper cluster in the same upward angle as the film
- Free

**14. Asahi Inari Shrine (朝日稲荷神社)**

- 📌 3-8-10 Ginza, Chuo-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Ginza Station (various lines)
- Asahi Inari Shrine is an especially important location in Weathering With You — it also appeared in Your Name when Taki dated Okudera-senpai
- A tiny shrine tucked inside a Ginza building — easy to miss and deeply atmospheric
- Free

**15. Busta Shinjuku (バスタ新宿)**

- 📌 5-24-55 Sendagaya, Shibuya-ku (Shinjuku Station South Exit)
- 🚇 Shinjuku Station south exit — directly connected
- Busta Shinjuku appears as the journey's end and a starting point for a new step in the film's ending — the 4th floor bus platforms and 3rd floor waiting areas overlook Tokyo in a composition matching the movie
- Free to enter and walk around

## 🚪 SUZUME (すずめの戸締まり) — 2022

_Suzume travels across Japan from south (Miyazaki) to north (Tohoku). The Tokyo section is the most visitable._

---

### 📍 Tokyo Locations

**16. Hijiribashi Bridge (聖橋)**

- 📌 4 Chome Kanda Surugadai, Chiyoda City, Tokyo
- 🚇 Ochanomizu Station (JR Chuo Line) — 2 min walk; or Akihabara Station — 10 min walk
- Hijiribashi Bridge is the background model for scenes where Suzume searches for Souta — the train running on the elevated railway, the Kanda River, and the multilayered city structure closely resemble the depiction in the film
- The iconic "three trains at once" moment requires patience — rush hours 7:30–9am give the best chance of all three train lines crossing simultaneously
- Free — it's a public bridge

**17. Ochanomizu Station (御茶ノ水駅)**

- 📌 Kanda Surugadai, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Ochanomizu Station (JR Chuo / Marunouchi Line)
- Suzume reunites with her aunt in front of Ochanomizu Station — the station exterior and Kanda River views are faithfully reproduced
- The area around the station also appears in multiple transitional scenes
- Free

**18. Tokyo Station (東京駅)**

- 📌 Marunouchi, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Tokyo Station
- The majestic Tokyo Station building contributed to the important atmosphere of Suzume — the Marunouchi brick facade appears in travel/arrival scenes
- Free to view and walk around

**19. Shinjuku West Exit Skyscraper District**

- 📌 Nishi-Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Shinjuku Station west exit
- The towering grey skyscrapers of Nishi-Shinjuku appear as the backdrop for key Tokyo scenes — the scale and perspective match the film
- Free

---

### 📍 Outside Tokyo — Suzume's Journey

**20. Nichinan Coast — Miyazaki (Kyushu)**

- 📌 Nichinan City, Miyazaki Prefecture
- Suzume's hometown is modelled on Nichinan City — the rugged coastal scenery, Udo Jingu seaside shrine, and blue Pacific coastline all appear in the opening act of the film
- ⚠️ _Not on your current itinerary — Kyushu / Miyazaki_

**21. Kobe — Transit Arc**

- 📌 Kobe City, Hyogo Prefecture
- Suzume travels through Kobe during her journey — Kobe appears as a transit city in the middle section of the film
- **Already on your itinerary as a day trip from Osaka (Oct 13)** ✅
- The Kobe harbour area and elevated expressways appear in passing scenes

**22. Naruto Whirlpools / Onaruto Bridge — Tokushima**

- 📌 Naruto City, Tokushima Prefecture (Awaji Island bridge)
- Suzume crosses Onaruto Bridge, a prime observation point for Tokushima's Naruto whirlpools — these form in the strait between Awaji Island and Shikoku
- ⚠️ _Day trip from Osaka possible (~1.5h by bus via Awaji Island) but not on current itinerary_

## 📐 5 CENTIMETERS PER SECOND (秒速5センチメートル) — 2007

_The earliest and most melancholy of Shinkai's films. Set across Tokyo and Tochigi._

---

### 📍 Tokyo Locations

**23. Shinjuku Station**

- 📌 Shinjuku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Shinjuku Station
- Shinjuku Station and the NTT Docomo Yoyogi Building appear prominently — the adult Takaki wanders through Shinjuku after parting ways with his lover
- The west exit walkways at dusk match the film's mood perfectly

**24. Sangūbashi Park (参宮橋)**

- 📌 Sangubashi, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo
- 🚇 Sangubashi Station (Odakyu Line)
- Sangūbashi Park appears in the film's background sequences — the quiet park canal paths near the Odakyu line feel exactly like the film
- Free; a genuinely quiet and overlooked park

**25. Mount Iwafune (岩船山) — Tochigi**

- 📌 Tsuruga-shima City, Tochigi Prefecture
- The rural train journey scenes in "Chapter 1: Oukashou" are set around Tochigi — the snow-covered rice fields and single-track train lines are real locations
- ⚠️ _Not on current itinerary — far north of Tokyo_

## 📸 Photography Tips

- **Suga Shrine (Your Name)**: Arrive 6:30–7:30am. By 10am there are queues. The exact film angle is from the bottom of the steps looking up.
- **Hijiribashi Bridge (Suzume)**: Best light is morning. Rush hour 7:30–9am gives the best chance of catching all three train lines crossing simultaneously — some visitors wait 15+ minutes for the perfect moment
- **Koenji Hikawa Shrine (Weathering With You)**: Visit in the evening — the lanterns create the exact atmospheric light from the film
- **Asahi Inari Shrine**: It's hidden inside a Ginza building. Easy to walk past — look for the torii gate on the ground floor

- _Your Name_: Nandemonaiya, Sparkle, Zenzenzense
