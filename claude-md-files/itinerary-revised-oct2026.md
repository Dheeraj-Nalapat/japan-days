# 🗾 Japan 2026 — Revised Itinerary (Option C · No Okinawa)

**Oct 11 – Oct 24, 2026 · 13 nights · Solo · Budget ₹2,00,000**
**Route:** Kochi → Bangkok → **Osaka → Koyasan → Kyoto → Hiroshima/Miyajima → Tokyo** → Bangkok → Kochi

> **What changed from the original plan**
> - ❌ **Okinawa dropped** — diving was the only reason to go, and a safe dive trip needs 2 nights minimum (no-fly-after-diving rule). Reinvested those nights + ~₹11k of internal-flight money into the mainland.
> - 🦌 **Nara + Kobe un-crammed** — Nara gets its own relaxed day; Kobe moves to the Koyasan-return afternoon.
> - ⛩️ **Kyoto: 2 → 4 nights** — the scattered temples are now split across days instead of one forced march.
> - 🏝️ **Hiroshima → Miyajima overnight** — sleep on the island, get the floating torii at dawn after day-trippers leave.
> - 🚄 **JR Pass killed** — the route doesn't justify the ¥50,000 nationwide pass. Buy point-to-point (see Rail section).
> - 🎬 **Anime pilgrimage woven in** — a half-day Makoto Shinkai route on a Tokyo day.

---

## 🗓️ Day-by-Day

### 📍 OSAKA — base: Hommachi / Shinsaibashi (Oct 11–15)

**Day 1 · Oct 11 (Fri) — Arrival**
- Arrive KIX 18:25 → Nankai Rapi:t / Haruka Express to the city (~60 min, ¥1,450–1,880)
- 🌙 Dotonbori (Ebisu-bashi, Glico sign, takoyaki at Kukuru)
- 🕯️ Hozenji Yokocho — lantern-lit mossy alley beside the chaos

**Day 2 · Oct 12 (Sat) — Osaka City**
- 🍁 Osaka Castle Grounds + Nishinomaru Garden (¥200, arrive 9am)
- ☕ Nakazaki-cho — bohemian machiya cafés, near-zero tourists
- 🐟 Kuromon Ichiba Market (lunch)
- 🍢 Shinsekai from 6pm — kushikatsu at Daruma (no double-dipping)

**Day 3 · Oct 13 (Sun) — Nara (relaxed full day)** ⭐ *de-crammed*
- 🦌 Nara Deer Park (Kintetsu from Namba, 40 min, ¥680)
- 🏛️ Todai-ji — Great Buddha hall
- 🌿 Isuien Garden — "borrowed scenery" framing Todai-ji's roof
- Back to Osaka at an easy pace — no Kobe rush today

**Day 4 · Oct 14 (Mon) — Koyasan overnight** 🛕
- Free Osaka morning (Denden Town or Tsuruhashi Koreatown)
- Afternoon (3–4pm): Nankai Koya Line Namba → Gokurakubashi → cable car → Koyasan (~2h)
- 🌲 Okunoin Cemetery at dusk — 2km lantern-lit cedar path
- 🛏️ Shukubo (temple stay) — shojin ryori dinner, futon, 6am prayer

**Day 5 · Oct 15 (Tue) — Koyasan → Kobe** ⭐ *Kobe moved here*
- 6am morning prayer + vegetarian breakfast → back to Osaka by ~11am
- 🌊 Kobe afternoon: Kitano Ijinkan (Western residences) — also a *Suzume* anime transit-arc location
- 🥩 Kobe beef teppanyaki dinner (Sannomiya, lunch/early sets cheaper)
- Last Osaka night; prep to move to Kyoto

---

### 📍 KYOTO — 4 nights (Oct 16–19) ⭐ *was 2 nights*

**Day 6 · Oct 16 (Wed) — Travel + ease in**
- Osaka → Kyoto (~15 min by shinkansen, or ~45 min local)
- Drop bags, gentle afternoon: Nishiki Market
- 🏮 Pontocho Alley + Gion Shinbashi-dori at dusk

**Day 7 · Oct 17 (Thu) — Kurama → Kibune mountain trail** 🌲
- Eizan Railway from Demachiyanagi to Kurama (¥470)
- Hike through cedar forest past Kurama-dera, descend to Kibune
- ⚠️ **Kawadoko over-water dining platforms close mid-October** — Oct 17 is borderline. If the platforms are down, regular Kibune restaurants + the hike are still excellent. Reconfirm with Hirobun.

**Day 8 · Oct 18 (Fri) — West Kyoto**
- 🎋 Arashiyama Bamboo Grove + Tenryu-ji (go early)
- ✨ Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) + Ryoan-ji rock garden
- Optional: Jojakko-ji (moss + maple, almost empty)

**Day 9 · Oct 19 (Sat) — East Kyoto (unhurried)**
- ⛩️ Fushimi Inari at dawn (before 7am — upper trails are silent)
- 🏯 Higashiyama / Kiyomizu-dera + Sannenzaka lanes
- 🍵 Wind down in Gion

---

### 📍 HIROSHIMA + MIYAJIMA — 1 night on the island (Oct 20) ⭐ *island overnight*

**Day 10 · Oct 20 (Sun)**
- Morning: shinkansen Kyoto → Hiroshima (~1h40)
- ☮️ Peace Memorial Museum + Peace Park + A-Bomb Dome (allow 2.5–3h)
- Late afternoon: JR ferry to Miyajima → check in on the island
- 🦌 Evening: the island empties — quiet streets, illuminated Itsukushima torii
- Last ferry back to mainland ~22:14, so an overnight is easy

---

### 📍 TOKYO — 2 nights central (Oct 21–22) + 1 night Narita (Oct 23)

**Day 11 · Oct 21 (Mon) — Miyajima dawn → Tokyo**
- 🌅 Dawn at the floating torii (low tide vs high tide both photogenic)
- ⛩️ Itsukushima Shrine (¥300) + optional Mt Misen
- Midday: ferry back → shinkansen Hiroshima → Tokyo (~4h; **Mt Fuji on the right, seat E**, ~40 min before arrival)
- Evening: settle into Tokyo

**Day 12 · Oct 22 (Tue) — Old Tokyo + Anime pilgrimage** 🎬
- 🏯 Senso-ji (Asakusa) before 7am → Nakamise-dori
- 🐱 Yanaka Ginza — nostalgic old-town shopping street
- **Makoto Shinkai half-day route** (all free, public): Suga Shrine steps (*Your Name*) → Hijiribashi/Ochanomizu (*Suzume*, best 7:30–9am for trains) → Shinjuku footbridge / Busta (*Weathering With You*)
- 🌆 Shibuya Scramble + Shibuya Sky at sunset

**Day 13 · Oct 23 (Wed) — Modern Tokyo / optional Fuji → move to Narita**
- Option A (city): Tsukiji breakfast → Hamarikyu Gardens → Shimokitazawa (indie/vintage) / Koenji
- Option B (Fuji day trip): Fuji Excursion ltd express Shinjuku → Kawaguchiko (~1h55, ~¥4,200) for lake + Fuji views
- Evening: move to **Narita** and sleep at the airport (see lodging) — flight is 05:45 next morning

**Day 14 · Oct 24 (Thu) — Departure**
- ✈️ XJ 601 NRT → Bangkok DMK 05:45 (be at airport ~03:45)
- ✈️ FD 170 Bangkok DMK → Kochi 18:50 → 22:30 🏠

## ✅ Things to Book (with links)

**Rail** — *no nationwide JR Pass.*
- For this route, **point-to-point wins**: Kyoto → Hiroshima (~¥11,300 Hikari/Sakura) + Hiroshima → Tokyo (~¥19,440 Nozomi reserved) + local IC top-ups ≈ **~¥32k**. The Kansai-Hiroshima Area Pass (¥17k) only beats this if you **add a Himeji or other Sanyo-line side trip** — then buy the pass instead.
  - Hiroshima→Tokyo ticket (official, English): **smartEX** → https://smart-ex.jp/en/
  - Kansai-Hiroshima Area Pass (only if adding side trips): https://www.westjr.co.jp/travel-information/en/tickets-passes/jrwest-rail-pass/kansai_hiroshima/
- **ICOCA IC card** (₹1,140, incl. deposit): https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/howto/icoca/

**Koyasan**
- 🛕 **Shukubo temple stay** (book first — sells out): **https://koyasan-shukubo.net/en/**
- 🚞 Nankai **Koyasan World Heritage Ticket** (round trip + cable car + buses, ~¥3,140): https://www.nankai.co.jp/en_railway/ticket/koyasan

**Kyoto**
- 🍱 Kibune **kawadoko lunch — Hirobun** (reserve 2–3 days ahead; *confirm season for Oct 17*): https://hirobun.co.jp/
- 🚆 Eizan Railway (no reservation, ¥470): https://eizandensha.co.jp/en/

**Tokyo**
- 🌆 **Shibuya Sky** (timed slots; sunset sells out — ¥2,700 / ¥3,400 after 15:00): https://www.shibuya-scramble-square.com/sky/ticket/
- 🎨 Optional teamLab: [Planets](https://teamlabplanets.dmm.com/en) · [Borderless](https://www.teamlab.art/e/tokyo/)
- 🎬 Anime spots (Suga Shrine, Hijiribashi, etc.) — **free public places, no booking**

**Hiroshima / Miyajima**
- ⛩️ Itsukushima Shrine (¥300): https://itsukushimajinja.jp/en/admission.html
- ⛴️ JR Miyajima ferry (¥180; + ¥100 visitor tax on arrival): https://jr-miyajimaferry.co.jp/en/fare/

**Optional Mt Fuji day trip**
- Fuji Excursion ltd express (Shinjuku⇄Kawaguchiko, ~¥4,200): https://e.fujikyu-railway.jp/fujikaiyuu/
- or Hakone Free Pass (¥7,100/2-day): https://odakyu-global.com/passes/hakone-freepass/
