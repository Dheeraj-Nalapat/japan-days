Day by Day

Full Itinerary

🏯
Osaka
大阪
Oct 11 – 12
2 nights
DAY 1
Oct 11

Arrival — Arrive KIX 18:25 local

Transit Haruka Express — KIX to Namba/Tennoji (~75 min, ¥1,880). Check in to hostel in Namba or Shinsaibashi area.
Evening Dotonbori — iconic neon canal. Try Takoyaki at Kukuru 🐙
Hidden Hozenji Yokocho — a lantern-lit mossy alley beside a tiny moss-draped temple. Far quieter than the Dotonbori chaos just 50m away.
DAY 2
Oct 12

Quiet Osaka

Morning Osaka Castle (¥600) — arrive before 9am, walk the Nishinomaru Garden for autumn foliage 🍁
Hidden Nakazaki-cho — Osaka's most bohemian neighbourhood. Vintage coffee shops, old wooden townhouses, almost zero tourists. Perfect afternoon wander.
Food Kuromon Ichiba Market — lunch with fresh sashimi and grilled scallops straight from the stalls
Evening Shinsekai — retro 1950s district. Kushikatsu at Daruma. 🍢
⛩️
Kyoto
京都
Oct 12 – 15
3 nights
DAY 3
Oct 12

Arrive Kyoto — Fushimi & Gion

Afternoon Fushimi Inari Taisha — hike past the main torii gates to Yotsutsuji intersection halfway up. Go after 4pm when day-trippers leave — the upper mountain trails become blissfully silent. Free, 24/7.
Hidden Shinbashi-dori in Gion — narrow stone-paved canal lane at dusk, lit by paper lanterns. Less photographed than Hanamikoji but more atmospheric. 🏮
DAY 4
Oct 13

Kurama & Kibune — Secret Mountain Villages

Very Quiet Kurama → Kibune trail — Eizan Railway to Kurama (40 min). Hike through ancient cedar forest past Kurama-dera Temple, then descend into Kibune village. Almost no foreign tourists. Lunch at riverside kawadoko terrace restaurant above the stream. 🌲
Evening Pontocho Alley — Kyoto's narrow riverside dining street. Kaiseki or yakitori dinner. 🍶
DAY 5
Oct 14

Arashiyama + Classic Temples

Early AM Arashiyama Bamboo Grove 🎋 — arrive before 7:30am for silence, mist, and the ethereal early light. Completely different from the crowd-filled daytime.
Hidden Jojakko-ji Temple — a 5-minute walk from the bamboo grove that almost nobody visits. Moss-covered stone steps winding through maple trees. More atmospheric than Kinkaku-ji with a fraction of the visitors. 🍂
Afternoon Kinkaku-ji Golden Pavilion (¥500) + Ryoan-ji rock garden
DAY 6
Oct 15

Eastern Kyoto + Nara Day Trip

Morning Kiyomizu-dera (¥400) + walk Ninenzaka & Sannenzaka stone lanes
Midday Train to Nara (45 min). Deer park 🦌, Todai-ji giant Buddha (¥600), Kasuga Taisha shrine
Hidden Isuien Garden — a hidden two-section strolling garden in Nara that most visitors skip. Uses "borrowed scenery" to frame Todai-ji's roof perfectly from within. ¥1,200. 🌿
☮️
Hiroshima
広島
Oct 16 – 17
2 nights
DAY 7
Oct 16

Hiroshima — Peace + History

Morning Shinkansen Kyoto → Hiroshima (~1.5h, JR Pass ✅). Peace Memorial Museum (¥200) — deeply powerful, allow 2–3 hrs. Peace Park & Atomic Bomb Dome.
Quiet Shukkei-en Garden — a 400-year-old miniature landscape garden in the heart of Hiroshima. Serene, almost always uncrowded, beautifully kept. 🌊
Dinner Okonomi-mura — 6-floor building of Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki stalls. Each floor has its own family-run shop. 🥞
DAY 8
Oct 17

Miyajima Island

All Day JR Ferry to Miyajima (JR Pass ✅). Itsukushima Shrine floating torii at high tide ⛩️. Hike Mt. Misen (1.5h) for sea views.
Hidden Gem Daisho-in Temple — at the foot of Mt. Misen, past the tourist path. Spinning prayer wheels, stone Buddhas in knitted hats, multi-level sub-temples. Rarely crowded. 🙏
Try Momiji Manju (maple leaf cakes) 🍁 and freshly grilled oysters 🦪 from island stalls
🌊
Okinawa
沖縄
Oct 18 – 21
3 nights
DAY 9
Oct 18

Arrive Okinawa — Naha & Shuri

Afternoon Shuri Castle 首里城 — the vibrant red-lacquered palace of the Ryukyu Kingdom. A UNESCO World Heritage site with an aesthetic utterly distinct from mainland Japan. (¥400) 🏯
Evening Makishi Public Market — choose fresh seafood from the 1F stalls, take it upstairs to a 2F restaurant who cooks it for you. A genuine Okinawan tradition. 🐟
DAY 10
Oct 19

Limestone Cliffs & Hidden Caves

Very Quiet Cape Manzamo (万座毛) — dramatic limestone cliffs carved by the sea into an elephant-trunk arch. Wild flowers, free entry, morning crowds near zero. Arrive at sunrise for the full drama. 🌊
Hidden Ryugudo Cave — 450m illuminated limestone cave inside a hill in central Okinawa. Atmospheric, rarely visited by foreign tourists. ¥1,200
UNESCO Katsuren Castle Ruins — hilltop Ryukyuan fortress with 360° panoramic views over the turquoise sea. UNESCO listed, almost always uncrowded. Free outer grounds. 🏰
DAY 11
Oct 20

Beaches + Churaumi Aquarium

Morning Manza Beach / Moon Beach in Onna Village — Okinawa's crystal clear water 🏖️. October is still warm (~26°C) and far less crowded than peak summer.
Afternoon Churaumi Aquarium (美ら海水族館) — one of the world's largest, with an 8.2m deep tank housing whale sharks (¥2,180). The adjacent Emerald Beach inside the park is free and stunning.
DAY 12
Oct 21

Sacred Forest Shrine + Depart for Tokyo

Morning Himeyuri Peace Museum — memorial to student nurses in WWII. Quieter and more intimate than Hiroshima's museum. ¥310
UNESCO · Sacred Sefa-utaki (斎場御嶽) — Okinawa's most sacred Ryukyuan shrine, hidden in a lush forest of ancient banyan trees. Enter through mossy limestone rock formations into a space that feels genuinely ancient. ¥300. 🌳
Evening ✈ Fly OKA → NRT with Jetstar Japan (~3 hrs). Arrive Tokyo night.
🗼
Tokyo
東京
Oct 21 – 24
3 nights
DAY 13
Oct 22

Old Tokyo — Asakusa & Yanaka

Morning Senso-ji Temple — Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple. Arrive before 7am to see it in the mist, before tourists arrive. Nakamise-dori shopping street.
Very Quiet Yanaka Ginza — Tokyo's most nostalgic old shopping street. Tofu shops, sembei cracker stalls, cats dozing on rooftops. Almost no tourists despite being walkable from central Tokyo. 🐱
Quiet Koenji evening — Tokyo's indie/bohemian district. Antique shops, vinyl record stores, great izakayas, zero tourist vibe. 🎸
DAY 14
Oct 23

Modern Tokyo + Last Shopping

Breakfast Tsukiji Outer Market — sushi breakfast at 7am 🍣. The inner market moved to Toyosu, but the outer market remains vibrant and authentic.
Hidden Hamarikyu Gardens — a 300-year-old shogunate garden surrounded by skyscrapers. A surreal, peaceful contrast in central Tokyo. ¥300 🏯
Afternoon Shibuya Scramble Crossing + Shibuya Sky observation deck at sunset (¥2,000)
Quiet Shimokitazawa — Tokyo's indie music and vintage fashion neighbourhood. No chain stores, no tourists, great record shops and pour-over coffee. 🎶
